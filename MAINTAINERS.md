# Maintainers

Minimal Router OS is currently maintained by a single project owner.

## Current maintainer

| GitHub | Role | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| [@vladimirperovic](https://github.com/vladimirperovic) | Project owner and maintainer | Product direction, architecture, security, backend, dashboard, packaging, documentation, and releases |

Repository path ownership is also declared in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- protect the project's security and recovery invariants;
- review changes proportionally to their privilege and failure impact;
- keep public documentation aligned with current behavior;
- avoid publishing private network data, credentials, or unreviewed artifacts;
- keep releases reproducible and evidence-based;
- respond to community contributions respectfully and without guaranteed SLA;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access is earned through sustained participation, sound technical
judgment, respectful review, reliable handling of sensitive information, and a
clear understanding of [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

A future maintainer normally progresses through scoped review responsibility
before receiving broad write or release access. Security-advisory and release
permissions may remain more restricted than ordinary code-review permissions.

## Inactivity and emeritus status

A maintainer who no longer has time to participate may move to emeritus status.
Emeritus maintainers retain attribution but do not hold active review, security,
or release responsibility.

## Bus-factor note

The project currently has a bus factor of one. Until additional trusted
maintainers are established, users should treat response times, release cadence,
and continuity as best effort. This limitation is one reason the project does
not currently claim commercial or unattended production support.
