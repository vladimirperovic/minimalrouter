# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Minimal Router OS is intended to be a welcoming project for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Maintainers and contributors
commit to making participation respectful, constructive, and free from
harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient and specific when explaining technical concerns.
- Assume good intent while still reviewing security-sensitive work carefully.
- Welcome questions from beginners and avoid gatekeeping.
- Critique code, documentation, designs, and decisions—not people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and security implications when relevant.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's credentials, network details,
  logs, private messages, or identifying information.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a change to preserve project scope or
  safety, and discuss that decision without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, repeated disruption, personal insults, and publishing private or
security-sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
review comments, community chat spaces, and public interactions where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include vulnerability
details in a public conduct report.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request changes in behavior, temporarily
limit participation, or permanently remove participants when necessary. Reports
will be handled as privately and consistently as practical.
